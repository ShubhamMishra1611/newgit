# Round 2 — direct attack on the core

Round 1 (see ../REPORT.md) did not solve either mission problem. It did, however, reduce each problem to one or two loose ends sharp enough to compute. Round 2 attacks exactly those.

Same rules as round 1 (../BRIEF.md): no fabricated results; every claim is labelled ESTABLISHED, CONJECTURE or OUR ATTEMPT; a fresh adversarial reviewer checks every OUR ATTEMPT claim.

## R2-T1 — Close the last loophole in the gravity bootstrap (mission 1, flat space)
- **Loophole.** 4d N=8 tree-level EFTs with factorization at 4, 5 and 6 points, positivity and "peculiar parity" (2607.14230) admit two corners: Virasoro–Shapiro (VS) and an infinite-spin-tower (IST) amplitude. Removing the IST currently requires assuming finite spin at the first massive level.
- **Attack.** Test the IST against independent consistency conditions:
  - causality / Shapiro time delay in the eikonal regime (CEMZ 1407.5597);
  - classical Regge growth (1910.14392);
  - partial-wave unitarity in D > 4;
  - the existence of a consistent 5-point residue;
  - a finite density of states.
- **Payoff.** If any of these kills the IST, then tree-level string theory is the unique consistent weakly coupled maximally supersymmetric gravity S-matrix under IR assumptions alone.

## R2-T2 — Does consistency tie Λ to Standard Model parameters? (mission 2; done by the lead, not delegated)
- **Mechanism.**
  - Compactify the Standard Model on a circle; the 3d radion potential is Λ₄ plus one-loop Casimir energies (ADNV hep-th/0703067).
  - The non-SUSY AdS conjecture (1610.01533) forbids stable non-SUSY AdS₃ vacua.
  - Together these constrain the neutrino masses relative to Λ (1706.05392, 1707.06326).
- **Attack.**
  - Reproduce the published bounds.
  - Update them with current oscillation data and the DESI DR2 bound on Σm_ν.
  - Decide whether our vacuum (Λ_obs, the measured neutrino sector) is consistent, and what the conjecture then predicts.

## R2-T3 — Settle the warped-conifold question for the KKLT dS candidates (mission 2, existence of dS)
- **Problem.** Round 1's T2.3 ("no conifold minimum") is conditional on the off-shell warp-factor model.
- **Attack.** 2512.17995 derives the warped off-shell potential from 10d and proposes a warped Kähler potential. Apply it to the conifold modulus plus anti-D3 of the 2406.13751 vacua, to decide whether a minimum exists.

## R2-T4 — Test a candidate non-perturbative definition of dS₃ gravity (mission 1, outside AdS)
- **Problem.** 2501.01486 (Collier–Eberhardt–Mühlmann) matches pure dS₃ gravity to a matrix integral.
- **Attack.** Check its output against the exact one-loop sphere partition function (2009.12464; Anninos–Denef–Law–Sun). Determine whether the invariant one-loop data are outputs of the proposal or inputs to it.
